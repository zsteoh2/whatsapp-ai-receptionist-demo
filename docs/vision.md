# Vision

Build a credible, clean demonstration of ORA, a reusable WhatsApp assistant for service businesses. A prospect should be able to scan a QR code, understand ORA's capabilities, request a callback, and contact the Founder. Once approved business-template data is supplied, the same system can activate service enquiry, booking, test payment, and calendar confirmation without changing ORA's identity.

The product is a demonstration for in-person, scheduled, or recorded presentations. It is not a production business system and must not present fictional example data as belonging to a real prospect.

## Problem

Give prospects a short, polished WhatsApp experience that demonstrates enquiry handling, booking, payment, calendar confirmation, and human follow-up without exposing implementation details.

## Target Users

- Business owners evaluating a WhatsApp automation service.
- Sales staff demonstrating ORA by QR code in shops, clinics, and other appointment-based businesses.

## Long-Term Vision

Support multiple industry-specific content templates without duplicating the proven conversation, calendar, payment, and messaging layers.

## Success Criteria

- A new prospect can understand ORA's purpose from the opening message.
- ORA never exposes the disabled Clinic template or invents replacement business facts.
- Once a business template is approved, natural enquiries and bookings remain reliable across common UK language styles.
- Completed demonstrations end with a clear path to retest or contact the Founder.

## Non-Goals

- Production multi-tenant configuration before a real cleaner-business process is supplied.
